05 / EVIDENCE REGISTER

Receipts before a name.

Scope, refresh and attribution rules

Scope. Public Coinbase trades do not expose customer IDs. This investigation follows a public lead: one relay cited in coverage of MARA and a second address observed co-spending with it. Neither a co-spend nor a transfer to a custodian establishes who sold BTC.

On-chain accounting. Transactions are deduplicated across both address histories. Fees, return outputs, external funding, and hub outputs are calculated separately in integer satoshis. Mixed inputs yield funding bounds. Without verified destination labels, Coinbase deposits are unknown.

Inventory. The watched balance excludes unrelated hub inventory. The treasury arithmetic uses reported holdings minus disclosed later sales and does not count previously pledged BTC again. Unreported production, later sales, and other balance changes are outside that calculation.

Refresh. The collector is scheduled hourly. The browser rechecks the published snapshot every ten minutes while visible. Failed sources keep the most recent observations with their original dates.
