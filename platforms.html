06 / SOURCE RULES

Comparable facts. Explicit differences.

Scope. Position comparisons follow exactly the twenty highest-volume pairs in the latest XYZ snapshot. Activity lists use each source’s broader published universe. Unsupported synthetics stay unmapped. Stock, ETF, CFD, index-reference, and perpetual contracts remain distinct instruments.

eToro. Only public-market and named public-investor endpoints are queried using read-only credentials in GitHub Actions. No private account positions or balances are collected. Public portfolio investmentPct and position netProfit are percentage points; v2 ranking gain is a decimal fraction. Dollar PnL is not inferred. The instrument buy/sell-holdings fields do not document a dollar-notional denominator.

Robinhood. The published Investor Index top ten supplies ranks, not ticker-level dollar weights, executed volume, or all customer holdings. Its methodology excludes ETFs, options, penny stocks, and crypto. Shared Social posts are not treated as a complete portfolio feed.

Schwab. STAX supplies a monthly overall score and a published table of top net buys/sells. Only table entries are used for ticker direction; mentions in the narrative do not become trades. An unlisted ticker’s direction is unknown.

IBKR. No IBKR account/market scanner is connected. Its documented position endpoints apply to authorized accounts, while market scanners describe market-wide activity—not all IBKR customers’ portfolios or broker-only execution volume.

Combined view. Select platforms to compare available facts. Source coverage is counted; incompatible measurements are not summed. People using multiple platforms cannot be deduplicated, so no “unique traders across all brokers” number is shown. Failed refreshes preserve last-good observations with their original dates.
