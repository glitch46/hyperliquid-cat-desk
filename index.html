How to read this desk

Two cohort lenses. All-time PnL and account-size cohorts overlap. Totals use only the PnL lens; never add both lenses together. Size bands are the source’s classifications, not independently recalculated balances.

Allocation. Dollar figures are gross open notional. Top-ten assets are reported by the source; “Other” is the remaining notional. Long share is long ÷ (long + short), calculated here rather than using the source’s equity-normalized bias field.

Different observation times. Cohort totals and cohort detail files are independent snapshots. Detail percentages use the detail file’s own denominator. Wallet position sheets are queried directly and can be newer than cohort aggregates.

Wallets, not people. Subaccounts and hedges elsewhere are not consolidated. A large short is an observed position, not evidence of a trader’s overall market view. Open-position profit share is not an historical win rate.

Refresh. This is a timestamped static snapshot, refreshed by the included workflow when deployed. Reload fetches the latest published file. Stale sections remain dated; unavailable data is not replaced with invented values.
