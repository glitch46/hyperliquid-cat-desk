How the numbers work

Current exposure, not traded volume. Long/short dollars are open position notional. Column order is 24h market volume; it is not how much each cohort traded. Each cell’s long share is long ÷ gross, and its net is long − short.

One classification. Only all-time perp-PnL cohort IDs 8–15 enter these totals. The same wallet’s size cohort is excluded. Missing cohorts remain unavailable; a reported zero gross value is explicitly flat.

Independent observations. Market quotes, per-pair cohorts, and public position samples update independently. Their observation times are displayed. Pair share is relative to this pair’s reported PnL-cohort gross, not margin, equity, or one-sided exchange open interest.

Bounded universe. The collector follows the top 20 active xyz pairs by 24h volume, retaining NVDA, XYZ100, and SP500 if those reference markets fall outside that set. “All tracked” shows this collected universe, not every listed xyz market.

Wallet samples. Cohort membership comes from the sample’s reported profile segment IDs. Wallets/subaccounts are not consolidated into people. A missing public sample cannot be used to infer a flat cohort.
